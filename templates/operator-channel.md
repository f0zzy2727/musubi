# Operator Channel

> **Append-only log of what Oya says *to you* — not a state surface.** Oya's
> pane is a stream: relay events from the pair keep arriving and push her
> answers up and out of view. Every message she addresses to you — answers to
> your questions, questions for you, status snapshots you asked for — is
> mirrored here verbatim, so you can always read it even after the pane has
> scrolled on.
>
> The channel viewer pane (added by `attach-oya.sh`) tails this file live.
> Blocking asks additionally land in `operator-actions.md` (the pinned state
> surface) — that file tells you *what's waiting on you*; this one carries
> *the conversation*. Entries are append-only and never edited after the fact.
